# Government policy documents go here

Drop PDF files directly in this folder.

## Where to get them
- Your own country's environment/climate ministry website (search
  "[country name] national climate policy PDF")
- UNFCCC NDC Registry (every country's official climate pledges):
  https://unfccc.int/NDCREG
- World Bank Climate Change Knowledge Portal: https://climateknowledgeportal.worldbank.org

## Suggested starting set (2-3 files is enough for Day 1)
1. One national climate policy or NDC (Nationally Determined
   Contribution) document
2. One regional/state-level sustainability policy, if your demo
   scenario is location-specific
