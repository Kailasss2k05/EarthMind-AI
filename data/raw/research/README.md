# General background research documents go here

Drop PDF files directly in this folder. This is the catch-all domain
for the Research Agent - general sustainability research, case studies,
academic papers, or background reports that don't fit neatly into
sdg/, environmental/, policy/, or finance/.

## Where to get them
- Google Scholar (search your demo topic + "sustainability case study")
- Sustainability-focused NGO reports (WWF, WRI - World Resources Institute)
- Academic PDFs on your specific demo scenario/region

## Suggested starting set
1-2 general reports or case studies relevant to your demo scenario.
